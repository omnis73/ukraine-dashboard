About and contact

Ragnar Bjartur Guðmundsson

I’m based in Iceland—the land of ice and snow, elves, and Björk! My work focuses on digital development and data analytics. My background is in economics and political science, and I hold an MBA from Reykjavík University.

I created this dashboard to make the daily figures on Russia’s war against Ukraine easier to follow, compare and trace back to their sources.

About this dashboard

The dashboard brings together daily reports and other published data about the war. It began as a Google Data Studio report and has grown to include historical comparisons, source notes and tools for exploring the figures.

I check figures from Ukraine’s General Staff against its published reports and add data from the other sources listed on the dashboard. The dashboard is updated at least twice a day, after the General Staff’s morning and evening reports. Other sections follow their own publication schedules.

This is an independent, non-commercial project that I maintain voluntarily. It is not affiliated with any government, military or organisation. I make no secret of my support for Ukraine, but I aim to present the available information accurately, including figures and developments that may be unfavourable to Ukraine.

Understanding and using the data

Each figure is labelled with its source. The General Staff’s figures are reported claims and are not independently verified. Third-party sources, estimates and my own calculations are identified separately. These figures can use different methods and should not be treated as interchangeable.

If you use a chart, figure or calculation from this dashboard, please credit and link to it, and name the original source shown with the data. For example:

Source: Russia’s war against Ukraine: daily data dashboard, by Ragnar Bjartur Guðmundsson, ukraine.vefgreining.com. Underlying source: [name of source].

For academic or formal work, cite the dashboard and the original source, and include the date you accessed the data. Some third-party data may have its own reuse terms, so check those terms before republishing it.

Daily figures cover the activity date shown on the dashboard, which is usually the day before the report was published. Totals and historical comparisons may change if a source corrects its figures. Estimates and calculations are labelled where they appear.
